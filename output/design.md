# Problem 10 — Yale Powerpuff Design

## Design direction

The site uses Yale navy as its foundation so the store still feels connected to campus. Powerpuff-inspired pink, sky blue, and lime green accents add playful energy to buttons, filters, chat, product highlights, and the battle game. Rounded cards, bold display type, high-contrast labels, and generous spacing keep the shopping experience readable while making the theme obvious.

## What changed

- Added a Yale navy, cream, pink, blue, and green visual system across the site.
- Added a themed “Powerpuff Campus Battle” section to the home page.
- Added original inline SVG campus-hero graphics in pink, blue, and green for the hero artwork, battle fighters, and character picker.
- Created CSS character cards for Blossom, Bubbles, and Buttercup-style heroes without requiring additional image assets.
- Added attack controls, health bars, battle feedback, reset behavior, and a visible victory state.
- Winning a battle reveals a merch discount code that the shopper can use as a promotional reward.
- Preserved product-card readability, detail-page navigation, and the floating chat widget while adding the theme.

## Why it helps customers and the business

The consistent colors and stronger visual hierarchy make products, prices, filters, and calls to action easier to scan. The battle gives visitors a short, optional reason to stay on the site and explore the collection. A clear win state and discount code turn that engagement into a concrete shopping incentive while keeping the core catalogue available to visitors who want to shop directly.

The battle is a front-end promotional interaction. It does not claim to apply a real checkout discount or change inventory; the displayed code is the reward to connect to a later promotion system.

The character art is original to this project and uses the theme's color language and simple superhero shapes rather than copied official artwork.
